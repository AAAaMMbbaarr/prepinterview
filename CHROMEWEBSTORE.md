## 2. Store Listing (Copy & Paste)

### Extension Name
PrepInterview Copilot - LinkedIn Job Match & AI Mock Prep

### Summary (Short Description - max 132 chars)
See your real-time Resume Fit Score directly on LinkedIn Jobs and launch instant AI mock interview practice in 1 click.

### Detailed Description (Max 16,000 chars - Plain text with line breaks)
Never walk into an interview unprepared. PrepInterview Copilot brings your interview readiness directly into your job hunt on LinkedIn.

When browsing jobs on LinkedIn, PrepInterview Copilot automatically evaluates your resume against the target role requirements in real time, displays an interactive Fit Score badge right on the job page, and lets you launch a tailored AI mock interview in a single click.

KEY FEATURES:

• Real-Time Resume Fit Score: Instantly see your match percentage, matched skills, and missing keyword gaps directly inside the LinkedIn job posting.
• Targeted Skill Breakdown: Highlights key technical, product, and leadership requirements extracted from the job description.
• Instant 1-Click Mock Prep: Export the exact job title, company, and role requirements directly into PrepInterview AI to start defending your resume immediately.
• Works Seamlessly: Embedded naturally into LinkedIn Jobs without interrupting your application workflow.
• 100% Client-Side Privacy: Your resume is cached locally in your browser storage. It is never sold, never uploaded to third-party ad networks, and never tracked.

HOW TO USE IT:

1. Click the PrepInterview icon in your Chrome toolbar.
2. Paste or save your resume text once.
3. Browse any job on LinkedIn (e.g., Software Engineer, Product Manager, Data Analyst, Growth Lead).
4. The PrepInterview Copilot panel appears directly on the job page with your Fit Score and skill breakdown.
5. Click Practice Spoken Mock Interview to immediately start defending your experience under pressure.

PRIVACY & DATA SAFETY:
PrepInterview Copilot respects your privacy. All text matching runs locally in your browser. We do not collect personally identifiable information, search histories, or credentials.
