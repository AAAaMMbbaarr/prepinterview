Privacy Policy Last Updated: September 2026

PrepInterview AI ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how our Chrome Extension (PrepInterview Copilot) and web platform (prepinterview.online) handle your information.

1. 100% Local Processing & Storage

PrepInterview Copilot operates with a privacy-first architecture:

  • Resume Data: Your resume text is stored strictly within your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local). It is never uploaded to any remote tracking server or sold to third-party recruiters.
  • Job Descriptions: The extension reads the job description on the active LinkedIn tab solely to calculate your local Skill Match and identify skill gaps on your device.

2. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Used solely to remember your saved resume across browsing sessions in your local browser profile.
  • *://*.linkedin.com/*: Required to read the active job posting description and render the interactive Skill Match overlay directly on LinkedIn jobs. No user cookies, credentials, or personal messages are ever accessed.

3. Web Application Data (PrepInterview AI)

When you click "Practice Spoken Interview", you are directed to https://prepinterview.online. The job description and role title are passed via standard secure URL parameters to pre-fill your interview practice session. Audio mock interview answers are evaluated in-session and are not retained for advertising.
